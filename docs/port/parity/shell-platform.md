Spec: `docs/port/specs/shell-platform.md`. Section 8 has 170 items, section 7 has bugs 1 to 23 plus the 7.3 absences, section 6 lists the tests to carry over. Every row carries its owner package (sp-1 to sp-5). Packages: sp-1 main window, app icon, tray, activation, recording handoff, ledger skeleton. sp-2 startup order, watcher lifecycle, close gate, global errors and logging. sp-3 cut-feature removal and audit of earlier modules. sp-4 shell chrome UI. sp-5 integration e2e, packaged smoke, live-copilot seam reconciliation, final ledger.
