Stop, and it writes.

The live text is only a preview. When you stop, Vozella goes back to the recording and makes the transcript properly, then writes the summary and action items.

  1. StopYou end the recording.
  2. TranscriptGenerated again from the full audio, with Speaker 1–4 labels if you turn them on.
  3. SummaryWritten by the engine you chose, from your template.
  4. Action itemsListed under the summary, ready to check off.

Why did we hold the monthly price?

Support tickets rose after the last change, so the team waited until after the launch on the 18th. See Pricing review.

Your meetings are files.

Each meeting is a Markdown file in your Documents folder, with its recording next to it. Open them in any editor, back them up however you like, and keep them if you ever stop using Vozella.

--- title: Pricing review --- ## Summary The team kept annual pricing as is and held the monthly change until after the launch. ## Action Items - [ ] Tell sales today - [ ] Send last quarter's ticket numbers

You choose who reads the transcript.

Transcription always runs on your PC. For summaries and chat, pick the engine you trust. Pick a cloud one and the transcript goes to that provider.
